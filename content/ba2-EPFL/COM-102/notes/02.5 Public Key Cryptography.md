The table following the torus rule will not always be filled.

The placing $[k]_{m_{1},m_{2}}\mapsto ([k]_{m_{1}},[k]_{m_{2}})$ is the action of a map
$$
\psi:\mathbb{Z} / m_{1} m_{2} \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z} / m_{1} \mathbb{Z} \times \mathbb{Z} / m_{2} \mathbb{Z}
$$
> [!Theorem] The Chinese Remainders Theorem
> When filling a table going down diagonally, we follow the torus rule
> If $m_{1}$ and $m_{2}$ are relatively prime, then the map $\psi$ is
> 1. bijective
> 2. an [[02.4 Commutative Groups#Isomorphism|isomorphism]] with respect to $+$ and $\cdot$
